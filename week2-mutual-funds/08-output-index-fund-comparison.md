# Chapter 2.8: Week 2 Output: Compare Nifty 50 Index Funds and Pick One

> **Goal:** compare two or three Nifty 50 index funds (direct plans) on the metrics that actually matter, choose one, and set up a SIP. This applies everything from Chapters 2.1–2.7.

Allow about 60 minutes.

## Step 1: Shortlist candidates

Every Nifty 50 index fund holds **the same 50 stocks in the same weights**. The only differences are **cost** and **execution quality**. That makes this an easy comparison.

Some well-established Nifty 50 index funds (verify current details yourself):

- ICICI Prudential Nifty 50 Index Fund

Pick **three** to compare.

## Step 2: Collect the data

### Where to look
1. **Value Research Online:** search the fund, pick the **Direct Plan – Growth** variant. Note the expense ratio, AUM, launch date and returns vs the index.
2. **AMFI website:** look for the **tracking error** and **tracking difference** disclosures for index funds (under the "Research & Information" or "Other Data" section). AMCs also show tracking error on their factsheets.
3. **AMC factsheet:** exit load, tracking error, fund manager.

## Step 3: Apply the decision rules

1. **Eliminate** any fund with:
   - AUM below about **₹500 crore** (small funds can have higher impact costs and tracking issues)
   - Less than **2–3 years** of history (not enough tracking data)
2. Among the remaining funds, choose the one with the **lowest tracking difference** over 1 and 3 years. Tracking difference is what you *actually lose* versus the index, since it captures the expense ratio **and** execution quality together.
3. **Tie-breaker:** the lower expense ratio, then the lower tracking error, then the larger AUM.
4. Don't agonise over differences of 0.02–0.05%. **Starting the SIP matters far more than picking the "perfect" index fund.**

### Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
| | Fund A | Fund B | Fund C |
|---|---|---|---|
| TER (Direct) | 0.18% | 0.10% | 0.06% |
| Tracking diff (1Y) | 0.22% | 0.14% | 0.19% |
| Tracking error | 0.03% | 0.04% | 0.09% |
| AUM | ₹20,000 Cr | ₹8,000 Cr | ₹1,200 Cr |

**Choice: Fund B.** It has the lowest tracking difference (0.14%). Fund C has the lowest TER but leaks more in execution, and its tracking error is higher. Fund A is the biggest but costs more.

## Step 4: Index fund or ETF?

If you have a demat account, you could also consider a **Nifty 50 ETF** (very low TER). Check:
- **Daily traded volume** (higher = better)
- **Bid–ask spread** and **premium/discount to iNAV** (should be tiny)

For a monthly SIP, the **index fund is usually simpler**: automatic SIPs at exact NAV, no spreads, no brokerage.
